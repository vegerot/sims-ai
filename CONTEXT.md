# The Sims 4 birthday mod

Language for a birthday gift that gives Sims contextual English dialogue and explores other AI features independently.

## Language

**Sim**:
A simulated character in The Sims 4.

**Active household**:
The group of Sims the human player currently controls.

**Actor**:
The Sim performing a particular social interaction.

**Target**:
The Sim toward whom a particular social interaction is directed.

**Trait**:
A game-maintained personality characteristic of a Sim, such as Neat or Bookworm.

**Mood**:
A Sim's current emotional state, such as Happy or Uncomfortable.

**Relationship track**:
A numerical measure of an aspect of the relationship between Sims, such as friendship or romance.

**Contextual dialogue**:
English dialogue between Sims that reflects their personalities, relationship, circumstances, and social interaction.
_Avoid_: Narration, world action

**Banter**:
A short exchange of contextual dialogue, with the playful energy of The Sims.

**Social interaction**:
An in-game social action involving Sims, such as flirting, telling a joke, or arguing.

**Player conversation**:
A conversation initiated through the human player's own words and addressed to a particular Sim.
_Avoid_: Banter

**Narration**:
Commentary describing game events from an observer's perspective, rather than dialogue attributed to a Sim.
_Avoid_: Contextual dialogue

**World action**:
A behavior attempted in the game, such as walking, hugging, or preparing food. A line describing a behavior is not itself a world action.
