# The Sims 4 birthday mod

Ubiquitous language for a birthday gift that replaces Simlish with spoken contextual dialogue in The Sims 4.

## Characters and Households

**Sim**:
A simulated character in The Sims 4, whether human or pet.
_Avoid_: Character, agent, entity, NPC

**Pet**:
A non-human Sim (dog, small dog, cat, horse, or fox) whose spoken line is an animal sound followed by a parenthetical inner-monologue translation.
_Avoid_: Animal object, companion

**Active household**:
The group of Sims the human player currently controls.
_Avoid_: Player party, active family

**Actor**:
The Sim initiating or performing a social interaction or solo vocalization.
_Avoid_: Source Sim, initiator, speaker 1

**Target**:
The Sim toward whom a social interaction or player conversation is directed.
_Avoid_: Recipient, listener, speaker 2

## State and Relationships

**Trait**:
A game-maintained characteristic of a Sim's personality, preferences, attractions, or quirks, such as Neat, Dog Lover, or Foodie.
_Avoid_: Attribute, perk, flag

**Pet quirk**:
A trait on a pet that describes a specific obsession, fear, or misbehavior, such as barking at the vacuum or begging for people food.

**Mood**:
A Sim's current primary emotional state, such as Happy, Playful, Confident, or Uncomfortable.
_Avoid_: Emotion, feeling

**Moodlet**:
A visible temporary buff on a Sim that contributes to their current mood and carries a specific cause, such as Home Sweet Home or Terrible Date.
_Avoid_: Status effect

**Relationship track**:
A numerical score measuring an aspect of the relationship between two Sims, such as friendship or romance.
_Avoid_: Affinity bar, meter

**Relationship bit**:
A discrete relationship fact or status between two Sims, such as Soulmates, Exes, Neighbor, or Good Friend.
_Avoid_: Relationship tag, link

**Sentiment**:
A directional short-term or long-term emotional impression one Sim holds toward another after a shared event, such as Bitter About Breakup or Adoring.

## Dialogue and Interactions

**Social interaction**:
An in-game social action between an Actor and a Target chosen by the simulation or queued by the player, such as Friendly Introduction, Flirt, or Joke.
_Avoid_: Affordance, mixer, social event

**Solo vocalization**:
An in-game vocal outburst performed by a single Sim with no Target, such as Shout Forbidden Words.
_Avoid_: Monologue, self-social

**Player conversation**:
A two-Sim exchange initiated when the player types what the active Sim says to a Target Sim on the lot.
_Avoid_: Direct chat, manual social

**Self-question**:
A one-Sim exchange initiated when the player asks the active Sim a question directly and the active Sim answers in the first person.
_Avoid_: Ask-self, solo chat

**Contextual dialogue**:
Spoken dialogue attributed to Sims that reflects their traits, moodlets, activities, relationship, and current interaction.
_Avoid_: Narration, world action, commentary

**Banter**:
A short, playful exchange of contextual dialogue between Sims.
_Avoid_: Chat log, script

**Pair memory**:
The rolling history of prior banter exchanges between a specific pair of Sims (or solo Sim) preserved across lots and sessions.
_Avoid_: Recent dialogue, zone history, chat history

**Narration**:
Commentary describing game events from an outside observer's perspective rather than lines spoken by a Sim.
_Avoid_: Contextual dialogue

**World action**:
A physical behavior or state change in the simulation, such as walking, hugging, or cooking. Spoken words are never a world action.
